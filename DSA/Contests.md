## How they fit the week
- **Weekly (Sun 08:00–09:30):** the long run ends at 07:30 and the German class starts at 14:00, so the slot is free. Eat and sit down by 07:55.
- **Biweekly (Sat 20:00–21:30):** it takes the Saturday "Friends" evening. Play live only when the evening is free. Otherwise do it as a **virtual contest** on Sunday afternoon or Monday, with a 90 min timer.
- Both are extra to the study template. They don't replace the Blind 75 blocks. Under the bad-sleep rule (<6 h), skip live and do the virtual one later.

## Rules
- Contests train speed under pressure. They are not the main way to learn a pattern: use the Blind 75 notes for that.
- Don't chase rating. Track "problems solved cleanly" and "pattern missed".
